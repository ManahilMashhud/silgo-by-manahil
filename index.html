About Silgo

Born from a passion for unique craftsmanship, Silgo is where artistry meets modern fashion. Our startup brings you a curated blend of two beautiful worlds: bespoke resin art and trend-setting motion bags.

The resin collection. We preserve the delicate beauty of nature within crystal-clear resin. From floral-infused earrings to personalized keychains and bracelets, each piece is a handcrafted keepsake designed to last forever.

Motion & beaded luxury. Staying ahead of the trends, we design exquisite beaded bags, commonly known as motion bags. With heavy beadwork and premium finishes, these bags are crafted to be the highlight of any bridal or party ensemble.

Handcrafted with heart. Every product at Silgo is handmade with meticulous attention to detail. We believe that true luxury lies in the imperfections and uniqueness that only "handmade" can offer.

At Silgo, our mission is to empower your personal style with accessories that tell a story. We don't just make jewelry and bags — we create wearable art that makes you stand out.
